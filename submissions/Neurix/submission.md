Short answers


1. What made you choose this problem?
We chose this problem because traditional education systems and standard learning management systems act as passive data vaults, leaving teachers overwhelmed with 30–60+ students and failing to provide personalized attention.
Students are left to navigate complex concepts in isolation, with rigid curricula that do not adapt to individual learning paces or real-world practical needs.
Specifically, engineering and medical fields face critical skill gaps—such as the lack of real-time coding troubleshooting and immersive clinical reasoning tools.
We built Mentora to solve these exact bottlenecks, transforming static learning into an active, AI-driven ecosystem that bridges theory with hands-on mastery.

2. Who is it for, and how would they use it in a normal week?
Mentora is built for students from grade 1- 12 , engineering and medical students as well as educators seeking personalized, tech-driven support. 
In a normal week, students would use it to upload their syllabus for custom 7-day study plans, troubleshoot coding challenges, and run immersive 3D clinical simulations. 
Meanwhile, educators leverage the 24/7 AI assistant to streamline lesson planning, monitor automated performance analytics, and manage administrative workflows effortlessly.

3. If you had another month, what would you build next?
If we had another month, we would expand Mentora beyond engineering and medicine by launching specialized, AI-driven learning modules for diverse professional and academic streams such as dental science, pharmacy, and MCA.
Additionally, we would introduce advanced peer-to-peer collaborative study rooms and deeper predictive analytics to further enhance personalized student mentorship.
we would also try to build a Mentora AI Desk Companion , A compact, physical desktop device featuring an e-ink display and voice activation that acts as a tangible 24/7 AI mentor, giving quick doubt reminders, visual progress ticks, and audio cues while studying.
